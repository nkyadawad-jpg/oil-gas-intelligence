## 🌟 Executive Overview

**Qatar O&G Opportunity Radar** is an intelligent, zero-hallucination commercial platform built to detect early procurement and project signals in Qatar before formal RFQs and public tenders are released.

Instead of waiting for competitive tenders to hit the market, the radar analyzes early EPC contract awards, plant lifecycle milestones, scheduled maintenance turnarounds, and government announcements across QatarEnergy, QatarEnergy LNG, QAFCO, Q-Chem, QAPCO, Pearl GTL / Shell, and major Tier-1 EPC contractors.

## 🚀 Core Features & Architecture

1. **📊 Executive Command Center**:
   - Monthly sales target tracking (`QAR 2.00M` target with real-time pipeline weighting).
   - OEM product portfolio allocation (Process Valves, Instrumentation, ASCO Solenoids, Protectoseal Flame Arrestors, Trillium Pump Spares, Westlock Positioners).
   - Real-time market signals stream and Tier-1 EPC activity feed.

2. **🎯 Project & Signal Radar (Unified Opportunity Lifecycles)**:
   - Tracks projects across Level 1 (Initial CAPEX) to Level 5 (Post-Award MRO).
   - Product applicability scoring (0–100) and pre-RFQ early engagement windows.

3. **⏰ Plant Shutdown & Turnaround Radar**:
   - Live countdowns for scheduled plant turnarounds (QAFCO-4 Ammonia, QE LNG Mega-Train 3, QAPCO Ethylene-2, Fahilah Compression).
   - Rapid replacement matching for valves, mechanical pump spares, and gauges.

4. **📑 Excel-Like CRM Data Grid**:
   - Full spreadsheet interface for tracking sales leads.
   - Inline double-click cell editing, new lead creation, CSV export, and persistent `localStorage` synchronization.

5. **🗺️ Interactive Qatar Industrial Facilities Map**:
   - Vector map covering all 6 industrial zones:
     * **Ras Laffan Industrial City (RLIC)**
     * **Mesaieed Industrial City (MIC)**
     * **Dukhan Oil & Gas Fields**
     * **Doha & Lusail Corporate HQ**
     * **Qatar Waters & Offshore Platforms (Bul Hanine, Ruya, NFB)**
     * **Doha Industrial Area & EPC Fabrication Yards**

6. **🏢 Tier-1 EPC Directory**:
   - Complete profiles for 10 Tier-1 EPCs (DOPET, TRAGS, Medgulf, QCON, Blackcat, Madina Group, Roots Energy, GDI, Al Balagh, Al Muftah).
   - Verified project awards, key decision-maker titles, and OEM product fit.

7. **🏭 Customer Accounts & PML Matrix**:
   - Preferred Manufacturer List (PML) pre-qualification status and Tawteen In-Country Value (ICV Grade A) alignment rules.

8. **📡 Live Government & Source Watcher**:
   - Connected to 12 official portals including QatarEnergy Mushtaryat, QE LNG Supplier Portal, QAFCO SAP Ariba, and MoF Monaqasat.

9. **✍️ AI Sales Outreach Generator**:
   - Generates customized pre-bid B2B emails, technical submittals, WhatsApp messages, and phone talk tracks in one click.

10. **🔍 Fact / Signal / Inference Audit & Arabic Source Drawer**:
    - Complete transparency: Separates hard public facts from market signals and technical inferences.
    - Preserves original Arabic tender text alongside English translations.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Single Page Application (SPA) built with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, FontAwesome 6, and Vanilla ES6 JavaScript (Zero external build dependencies).
- **Backend / Utilities**: Lightweight Python 3 HTTP Server (`server.py`) & Government Verification Engine (`sync_engine.py`).
- **Data Protocol**: Zero-Hallucination protocol, strict corroboration against official Qatar energy releases.

## 💻 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: Direct Browser Launch
Simply double-click or open `index.html` in any modern web browser.

### Option 2: Python Local Server
```bash
python server.py
```
*Launches local server at `http://localhost:8080` and opens your default browser.*
